Evaluation Basis: Internal Examiner Report (28 July 2025) and External Examiner Report (29 July 2025)

1. Overall Comment & Executive Summary

This critical review evaluates the revised Chapters 1–4 against the substantive requirements raised in the Internal and External Examiner Reports. The revised thesis shows substantial improvement. However, the examiner corrections are not fully complete. The most serious remaining issues are methodological and cross-chapter rather than cosmetic. The analytical sample is reported as both 160 and 169; the socio-demographic variables are described in Chapter 2 as categorical/descriptive indicators but are treated in Chapters 3–4 as a single latent/composite moderator despite poor reliability and heterogeneous content; Chapter 3 specifies a two-stage moderation procedure while Chapter 4 reports the product-indicator approach; and the original examiner concern about construct and questionnaire validity remains unresolved because several constructs contain conceptually mixed items and the broad economic dimensions are still represented by essentially one item each.
